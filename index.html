*PROTOCOL

How a Woof is born

From deploy to graduate — fully on-chain, no games.

01 DEPLOY

Deploy

Name, ticker $uWOOF, 5,000 supply, and curve were set permanently at launch. Collections use 18 decimals, so one whole token always backs one item.

02 LAYERS

Upload layers

Art goes on-chain across several transactions. Once trading opens, the layer set is locked. Metadata cannot change later.

03 REVEAL

Buy & reveal

A buy mints one Woof per whole $uWOOF token. Traits reveal layer by layer from a later block, so rarity cannot be gamed at buy time.

04 GRADUATE

Graduate

At the ETH target, anyone can graduate. Liquidity moves to Uniswap and the LP key is burned. Each Woof also trades on its own card.
